# Star Swarm: AdSense readiness notes
Prepared: 20 October 2026

## Included in this branch
- Expanded responsive CREDITS for desktop and mobile web, preserving current gameplay and hiding inactive DONATE.
- Linked About / How to Play page with PC keyboard and touch control instructions.
- Linked Privacy Policy with current localStorage, hosting and conditional video embed disclosures.
- Updated GitHub Pages deployment so both new pages are published with index.html.
- No advertising scripts or fabricated cookie settings were added.

## Requires site-owner action before enabling ads
1. Confirm legal publisher details and a private contact method for the privacy page; review the notice for completeness.
2. Confirm hosting domain and AdSense site approval, and provide the publisher ID only when enabling ads.
3. Set up a Google-certified CMP in AdSense Privacy & messaging (including the required applicable consent framework and geographic coverage). Integrate and verify that live consent controls work on PC and mobile.
4. Update the privacy notice for the exact installed ad partners, vendors, cookies and data flows.
5. Design actual ad placements away from gameplay and FIRE/arrow touch controls; test on real phones and desktop browsers.
6. Verify the deployed public site, all links and required Google site quality criteria. AdSense approval is not guaranteed.
7. Revisit the conditional YouTube video embed and its disclosures/consent as appropriate.
8. Keep DONATE inactive until a payment provider has been configured and disclosed.
