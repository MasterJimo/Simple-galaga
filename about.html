ABOUT STAR SWARM

A free arcade-style space shooter for desktop and mobile browsers.

Star Swarm is a single-player space shooter created by MrJim. Pilot a ship, dodge enemy fire and try to survive increasingly challenging waves. There is no player account or download required to play in a supported web browser.

How to play

Move left or right to line up shots and avoid incoming projectiles. Some enemies dive toward your ship. Clear every enemy in a wave to start the next one. You begin each run with three lives.

PC / keyboard controls

  • ← / → or A / D — move.
  • Space — shoot (hold to keep firing).
  • Enter — start or restart.
  • P — pause or resume; you can also use the PAUSE button in settings.

Phone / tablet controls

Use the on-screen FIRE, ◀ and ▶ buttons to shoot and move. Tap the game to begin. The game scales to smaller screens; the settings and credits panels appear below the play area so they do not cover the touch controls.

Points, waves and lives

You start with three lives. Contact with an enemy or an enemy projectile costs a life. Shooting enemies scores points: enemies in formation award 50–100 points depending on their row, and diving enemies are worth 150 points. Clearing all enemies advances to the next wave.

Sound and settings

Choose from ten original procedurally generated soundtracks. Change the music and effects volume separately, or mute the game. The selected soundtrack is remembered in local browser storage; see Data & Storage.

Availability

Star Swarm is currently free to play. Advertising and financial-support functions are not enabled. You do not need to provide payment details to play.
